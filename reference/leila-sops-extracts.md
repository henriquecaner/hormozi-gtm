## Framework 1: 5 Star Service

5 standards that set apart a company that charges premium:

1. **Concern** — you visibly care about the client's result
2. **Courtesy** — respectful communication even under pressure
3. **One-Done** — promises kept in one round-trip, no bureaucratic follow-up
4. **Consistency** — the same standard on call 1, call 100, call 1000
5. **Compensation** — when you mess up, you make it right proactively

## Framework 2: High-Performance Communication

3 pillars:

1. **Energy** — posture, pace, presence
2. **Engagement** — check for understanding, ask questions, confirm
3. **Exploration** — go past the surface, ask "why" 3 times

## Framework 3: Hiring & Onboarding

Core principles:
- Hire slow, fire fast
- A players attract A players; B players attract C
- Onboarding in the first 30 days sets the next 3 years
- Documented roles beat culture by intuition

## Framework 4: Delegation (75% Rule)

- If someone does the task at 75% of your quality, delegate it
- Waiting for 100% kills productivity
- Document the process before you delegate (no doc = not ready)

## Framework 5: Metrics & Accountability

- One primary metric per role (not 10 KPIs per person)
- Review cadence: weekly per team, monthly per business unit
- Clear ownership (one person per metric)
- A consequence tied to the metric (positive or negative)

- 5 Star Service in service SOPs
