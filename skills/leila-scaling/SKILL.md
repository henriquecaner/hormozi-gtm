# Leila's 5 Scaling Frameworks

Source: *Final of Leila Hormozi's 5 Scaling Framework SOPs*.

## Why this skill exists

Hormozi writes about offer, leads, money model — the selling side. Leila writes about the serving side and growing a team. A solid business plan needs both.

## Framework 1: 5 Star Service

The 5 service standards that set apart a company that charges premium:

1. **Concern** — you visibly care about the client's result. Not faked, demonstrated.
2. **Courtesy** — respectful communication even under pressure.
3. **One-Done** — promises kept in a single back-and-forth, no bureaucratic follow-up.
4. **Consistency** — the same standard on call 1, call 100, call 1000.
5. **Compensation** — when you mess up, you make it right proactively (you don't wait for the client to complain).

**Application:** the service promise on the LP aligns with these 5. Support SOPs are trained on these 5.

## Framework 2: High-Performance Communication

3 pillars for communication that lands:

1. **Energy** — posture, pace, presence in your delivery. Not monotone.
2. **Engagement** — check for understanding, ask questions, confirm.
3. **Exploration** — go past the surface, ask "why" 3 times.

**Application:** ad copy reflects High-Performance Communication (high energy, not generic AI). Closer training uses the 3 pillars as a metric.

## Framework 3: Hiring & Onboarding (summary)

Core principles (full detail in the original PDF):
- Hire slow, fire fast
- A players attract A players; B players attract C players
- The first 30 days of onboarding define the next 3 years
- Documented roles beat culture by intuition

## Framework 4: Delegation

The "75% rule":
- If someone does the task at 75% of your quality, delegate it
- The 100% you're waiting for never arrives — perfect is the enemy of done-by-someone-else
- Document the process before you delegate (no doc = not ready to delegate)

## Framework 5: Metrics & Accountability

Structure:
- One primary metric per role (not 10 KPIs per person)
- Review cadence (weekly by team, monthly by business unit)
- Clear ownership (one person per metric, always)
- A consequence (positive or negative) tied to the metric

## Operationalizing: a primary metric per framework

Without a metric, a scaling framework becomes "culture" — a pretty word with no behavior changed. Each framework needs at least one metric you can measure weekly.

| Framework | Primary metric | Cadence | Realistic target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. 5 Star Service** | % of promises kept on the 1st call + average response time under 24h | weekly | 90%+ promises / 4h response |
| **2. High-Performance Communication** | Client feedback on a 1-5 scale ("energy/clarity") in a post-call survey | per call | NPS-like ≥ 4.5/5 |
| **3. Hiring & Onboarding** | Day-30 completion rate (% who finish onboarding in 30 days) + 90-day retention | monthly | 80% onboarding / 90% retention |
| **4. Delegation** | Delegated task completion rate vs self-done (% of tasks done by the team vs the founder) | weekly | 75%+ delegated in a mature squad |
| **5. Metrics & Accountability** | 1 primary metric per role + % of roles with a weekly review actually run | weekly | 100% of roles with a review/week |

**Primary metric per role (B2B SaaS reference):**

| Role | Primary metric |
|---|---|
| Sales / SDR | Reply rate on outbound (cold) or MQL → SQL conversion (warm) |
| Customer Success | Net revenue retention + day-90 product adoption |
| Ops / Implementation | Time-to-value (days between signup and the client's 1st outcome) |
| Founder / CEO | LTV:CAC + cash runway (months) |
| Engineering | Deployments/week × post-deploy incidents |

Hormozi rule: if you can't measure it weekly, it's not a primary metric — it's a vanity metric.

## Application by use case

| Case | How to use Leila Scaling |
|---|---|
| Sales LP | Service promise (5 Star) stated explicitly. "Support with a One-Done Guarantee in 24h" |
| Ad script | Copy reflects High-Performance Communication — energy, engagement, not a generic tone |
| Business plan | "Operations" section maps hiring, onboarding, delegation roadmap, primary metrics per role |

## When this skill does NOT apply

- Offer audit (focuses on the Value Equation)
- LP for a digital product with no team (you are the team)
- Short-form ad script (focuses on the hook, not on ops)
