# Voice register — brutal Hormozi

This skill exists because describing the voice with adjectives ("be brutal, direct,
unfiltered") produces **average** copy. The model imitates examples, not adjectives. Load
this and **imitate the register** — copy the rhythm and the attitude, not the words.

## Output checklist (run BEFORE delivering — non-negotiable)

These are the levers that slip the most often. Before returning any headline/CTA, confirm
each item and rewrite whatever fails:

- [ ] **CTA is a COMMAND with a consequence**, not a wish. ❌ "I want to double my pipeline" / "Book your audit" → ✅ "Install the system. Or keep paying $X CAC forever." / "Stop outsourcing the conversation that closes. Start now." (A "I want…" CTA is a lukewarm wish — banned in the primary CTA.)
- [ ] **Risk reversal VISIBLE in the hero or the CTA**, not in the footer. "If it doesn't hit [number], I work for free until it does." / "Worst case: you cuss me out and take every dollar back."
- [ ] **At least 1 hammer line** — short, dry, alone on its own line.
- [ ] **Zero marketing adjectives** (bulletproof/predictable/transformational/next-level) — number and verb instead.
- [ ] **Brutality rubric score ≥7.** Below that, it doesn't ship.

## IMITATE this register exactly

These are Hormozi's real English patterns. Don't copy them literally — copy the structure:

- "I'll write you a check the day you tell me we suck." (conditional guarantee inside the CTA)
- "Fill Your Gym in 30 Days." / "Back Sore No More." (flat, non-metaphor outcome — names the thing they actually want)
- "We only accept 5 new clients per week. 3 spots are already taken." (honest scarcity)
- "-$22,000. Payment Successful. Balance: $1,036." (raw financial specificity, no spin)
- "I'm not the first-dollar guy. I'm the last-dollar guy you'll ever need." (Golden Ticket framing)
- "Most people don't have a lead problem. They have an offer so bad no lead can save it."
- "You posted on LinkedIn for six months. How much of it closed? I already know the answer."
- "You post. Two likes. One's your co-founder."
- "Waiting isn't a channel. It's hope."

**Calibrated example for a B2B SaaS founder (the kind of copy this voice should produce):**
"B2B SaaS founder: I put you in front of 5 decision-makers who fit your deal size in 30
days, using your own LinkedIn. If it doesn't happen, I work for free until it does. Worst
case: you tell me it was garbage and I hand back every dollar. The one thing that's
guaranteed NOT to help is closing this tab."

## Hard rules (non-negotiable in external copy)

1. **Zero marketing adjectives.** Banned: bulletproof, predictable, transformational, powerful, robust, scalable, strategic, optimized, innovative, disruptive, "next-level", "predictable machine", "locked-in channel". Use **number + verb**.
2. **First person with personal stakes.** "I put you in front of", "I work for free until it lands." Not institutional third person ("founders can install").
3. **Attack the reader's wrong belief BEFORE you offer.** Name the objection to their face ("You posted for six months. How much closed?"), don't pretend it isn't there.
4. **CTA is a command, not an invitation.** "Install your pipeline channel." / "Stop burning pipeline." NEVER "Book your free audit" / "Learn more" / "Talk to a specialist".
5. **Risk reversal visible in the headline/CTA itself**, not buried in the footer ("or you don't pay" / "I work for free until it hits").
6. **Outcome in plain language**, the thing they actually want ("5 meetings with decision-makers"), not a pretty metaphor ("a bulletproof pipeline channel").
7. **Hammer rhythm.** A short dry line, alone on its line, after a long one that builds tension. "Two likes. One's your co-founder."
8. **Posture of strength.** You offer and set a condition ("I take 5 a month"), you don't beg for the meeting ("book now!").

## Failure mode to avoid ("polished" copy)

These are real — generated by this plugin before the fix. Do NOT reproduce this register:

- ❌ "B2B founders: in 3 sessions and 15 minutes a day, install your expertise as a bulletproof LinkedIn pipeline channel." (metaphor, no first person, no risk, no confrontation)
- ❌ "BOOK A FREE AUDIT" (generic button, names no one, repels no one)
- ❌ "Discover how founders are unlocking predictability with a proven methodology." (vague attribution, unnamed mechanism)

## Brutality rubric (0–10) — release gate

Score every piece of external copy (headline/sub/CTA/body). +1 per criterion present:

1. First person with personal stakes (not institutional third person)
2. A raw, specific result number (5 meetings, $100k, 30 days) instead of an adjective
3. Risk reversal visible in the piece itself ("or you don't pay")
4. Outcome in plain language, not metaphor
5. Direct confrontation / objection named to their face
6. CTA names WHO and/or disqualifies — not a generic button
7. Short dry hammer line (≤5 words, isolated)
8. Posture of strength / honest scarcity — no begging

**Penalties:** −2 if it hits the criteria but is all metaphor/abstraction; −3 for "discover how / proven methodology / journey / next-level"; −3 for zero numbers.

**Bands:** 0–3 polished copywriter (redo) · 4–6 still smells like an agency · 7–8 real Hormozi · 9–10 grabs you by the collar.

**Gate:** external copy only ships at **≥7**. Below that, rewrite before delivering.

Calibration: "bulletproof pipeline channel" ~3 · "BOOK A FREE AUDIT" ~1 · "I put you in front of 5 decision-makers in 30 days or I work for free until it lands" ~9.

## Language

This register is language-independent. When gtm-context sets `language` to a non-English
locale, produce the copy in that language while preserving every rule above — the bite,
the hammer rhythm, the named objection, the risk reversal in the CTA. A translated-sounding
version fails the rubric. Write it native in the target language, not translated from English.
