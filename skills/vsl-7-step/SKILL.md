# VSL 7-Step Arc

Source: Combines *$100M Offers* (sell mechanism) + *$100M Leads* (longform copy patterns).

## The structure

```
Hook → Story → Problem → Mechanism → Proof → Offer → CTA
```

A good longform VSL runs 8-15 min. Longer? There's fat to cut. Shorter? You're starving the mechanism and proof.

## Step 1: Hook (0:00-0:15)

**Job:** grab attention, make the viewer feel they have to keep watching.

3 options (use the `hook-framework` skill):
- Problem hook
- Dream outcome hook
- Secret hook

**Critical:** the first 3 seconds decide whether they stay. No empty promises.

## Step 2: Story (0:15-2:00)

**Job:** establish credibility through a personal or client narrative.

3 angles:
- **Origin story:** "How I figured this out after Y years of getting it wrong"
- **Client story:** "Client X had problem Y and we did Z"
- **Discovery story:** "I was solving A when I noticed B"

**Bar:** the story has to have real conflict, not an easy win.

## Step 3: Problem (2:00-4:00)

**Job:** the viewer recognizes their own problem in painful detail.

Structure:
- 3-5 concrete symptoms of the problem
- Why the usual solutions fail
- The real cost of NOT fixing it (in dollars, time, or opportunity)

**Bar:** the viewer thinks "how does he know this about me?"

## Step 4: Mechanism (4:00-7:00)

**Job:** reveal why YOUR method works — named and explained.

Structure:
- A proper name for the method (not "my approach," but "the R.A.M.P. System")
- 3-5 components of the method (not 12)
- Why it's different from the alternatives
- A counterintuitive insight (something that looks wrong but works)

**Bar:** the mechanism passes the "why" test. Why does this work when the others don't?

## Step 5: Proof (7:00-9:00)

**Job:** break skepticism with specific evidence.

Structure:
- 3-5 case studies comparable to the viewer's ICP
- Explicit numbers (before/after)
- Diversity (not 5 case studies from the same profile)
- Real photo/name when possible

**Bar:** the viewer flags at least 1 case study as "that could be me"

## Step 6: Offer (9:00-11:00)

**Job:** present the offer as a Grand Slam Offer.

Structure:
- What's included (named deliverables)
- Bonus stack (3-5, an odd number)
- Total perceived value vs price charged (10:1 target)
- Conditional performance guarantee
- Genuine scarcity

**Bar:** the viewer thinks "this is worth way more than it costs, by an absurd margin"

## Step 7: CTA + Urgency (11:00-12:00)

**Job:** give a specific verbal action + a genuine reason to act now.

Structure:
- Specific verbal action ("click the link and answer 3 questions")
- Reason to act now (real deadline, next cohort, fast-action bonus)
- What happens next ("you land on an application page, I see it, I reply within 24h")
- 30s recap of what's being offered

**Bar:** the viewer knows EXACTLY what to click and what happens next.

## Anti-patterns

- Hook → Offer with nothing between (skips 5 acts, kills conversion)
- 5-minute story (lost the viewer)
- Mechanism with no name (turns into a generic "my method")
- Proof with 1 case study (not enough)
- "Learn more" CTA (generic action)
- No real urgency (lets them decide later)

## Workflow

1. Write the **Offer** first (step 6) — everything else lines up behind it
2. Pick the **Hook** aligned with the dominant angle
3. Build **Story → Problem → Mechanism → Proof** that sell the Offer
4. Close with a specific **CTA**
5. Read it out loud with a timer — cut the fat
6. Run it through the `humanizer` subagent before delivering

## Application by use case

| Case | Use |
|---|---|
| Longform VSL script | Full structure, 8-15 min |
| Short-form script | Compress acts 3-7 into 30-60s |
| Sales LP | Structure mirrored across the page's sections |
